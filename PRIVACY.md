# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 16, 2026_

Family Health is a place to keep your own family's medical records. It is designed so that we never see them.

## What we collect

Nothing. Family Health has no server, no account sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet to send your information anywhere.

## Where your records are stored

Everything you enter, including photos of insurance cards, is stored only on your device. iOS encrypts the app's files whenever your device is locked, and the app can require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open.

Family Health does not sync records to iCloud. Like other app data, records are included in your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on; those backups are managed by Apple and your device settings, not by us.

## Sharing

Records leave your device only when you choose to share them, for example by exporting a PDF summary or a backup file and sending it with AirDrop, Mail or Files. You decide where those go.

## Camera and photos

The app asks to use the camera or your photo library only so you can attach pictures, such as insurance cards. Pictures stay on your device.

## Purchases

If you buy the one-time unlock in the App Store version, Apple handles the payment. We do not receive your payment details.

## Medical disclaimer

Family Health does not give medical advice. Always check with your doctor or pharmacist before making medical decisions.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support link on the App Store listing.
